# STOCK & FOUND 88 — MARKET EXPERIENCE V1

## North star
Recreate the *behavior* of the physical market online:
walk -> notice -> browse -> discover -> evaluate -> claim.

This is not a dark-pattern system. Scarcity, availability and social signals must be truthful.

## Behavioral principles
1. Curiosity: lead with "what was found today", not a static catalog.
2. Choice reduction: a small number of clear paths before exposing the full catalog.
3. Progressive disclosure: reveal detail as intent increases.
4. Visual salience: hero find + two supporting finds create a visual "stall".
5. Goal gradient: FIND -> COMPARE -> CLAIM gives the journey a clear next step.
6. Variable discovery: "Surprise me" recreates the unpredictability of browsing a market.
7. Loss aversion, ethically: show "ALMOST GONE" only when actual stock supports it.
8. Recognition over recall: brand/category/price are visible at the decision point.
9. Autonomy: the user can browse, search, filter or be surprised; no forced funnel.
10. Trust: no fake timers, fake purchase notifications, invented reviews or fabricated scarcity.

## Experience architecture
HOME
- Market pulse
- "Today at the market"
- hero find
- two supporting finds
- random finds
- categories / brands
- market ↔ online connection
- Finder

THE HUNT
- sticky market chips
- all finds / just found / almost gone / edit / under €50
- surprise me
- catalog

FINDER
- I KNOW
- SHOW ME
- SURPRISE ME
- category / budget / keyword

PRODUCT
- product identity
- truthful availability
- size selection
- claim CTA
- related finds
- market origin / QR path when available

## Visual language
- warm paper background
- black ink
- thin rules
- oversized grotesk type
- mono labels
- asymmetric product compositions
- editorial + market photography
- motion used to guide attention, not decorate every section

## Commercial rule
Reference retail prices and demo prices are not client inventory.
Before launch, every product must have verified:
brand, model, image rights, stock quantity, sizes, selling price, status and source.
